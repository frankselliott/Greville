# /visual — source register

Every geometry, camera or control point the viewer displays traces to one entry here and one entry in `assets/manifest.json`. That manifest is generated by `tools/build_assets.py` and carries the per-asset SHA-256.

## 1. Coordinate system

| Item | Definition |
|---|---|
| Horizontal | GDA2020 MGA zone 56, **EPSG:7856**, metres |
| Vertical | Australian Height Datum (AHD), metres; "RL" |
| Source local frame | `x = E − 504045`, `y = N − 6966712`, `z = RL`; Z up; x east, y north. This is the frame of the archived visual scene (`crs` field of `scene_dump_v2.json`). |
| Viewer frame (Three.js) | `X = x` (east), `Y = z` (up, RL), `Z = −y` (north is −Z). This is a proper rotation (det +1) and is tested in `tests/unit.test.mjs`. |
| Site grid | The local frame rotated by 8.864°. Some source dimensions are stated in it as (u, v), with `x = u·cos θ + v·sin θ`, `y = −u·sin θ + v·cos θ`. |
| Camera angles | **Heading:** clockwise from grid north (+y), degrees. **Pitch:** up positive. **Roll:** clockwise positive about the view axis. **HFOV:** across the full frame width. Principal point `(cx, cy)` in pixels, with `cy = H/2 + shift·W/2`, where `shift` is the archived "vertical lens shift" in units of the image half-width. These are the conventions of the archived renderer and camera-fit core, ported unchanged. |

**Deliberately not used:**
- **Archive comparators from other families.** `E1_REC48` / `REC52` (an inverse fit), `E1_CUT48` (approved footprints cut to height) and `E1_SK46.5` are not exposed. The SK family is never mixed with REC or CUT.
- **Opaque tree sensitivity prisms.** `SENS_trees12_opaque` and `SENS_C5_verge_trees` are not shown, because they are invented crowns.
- **Laundry flat-top variant.** `SENS_C3_laundry_flat_top` is not shown either.
- **Shadow-model geometry.** The `model/` shadow-model geometry is not used (a different method; see `model/PROVENANCE.md`).
- **No façade detail.** There are no façade finishes, windows, screens, people or textures. The solids are schematic massing.

## 5. Publication and licensing review (for this public repository)

- **Published:** derived numeric massing (vertex coordinates and RLs), a DEM grid, canopy heights, control-point coordinates and the camera conventions.
- **Not published:**
  - any drawing image;
  - any photograph;
  - any private correspondence or legal document;
  - any third-party perspective image;
  - any private report.
- **Licences:**
  - The LiDAR-derived layers derive from State of Queensland data, CC BY 4.0, and are attributed in the viewer.
  - The building massing records dimensions measured from publicly lodged development-application drawings. No drawing content is reproduced.
  - Three.js is MIT.
- **Images loaded into the perspective-match mode stay in the browser** (object URL). They are hashed locally and never uploaded.
