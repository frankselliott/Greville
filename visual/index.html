What is accurate, and what is not

  • Geometry and camera are unchanged by any display setting. Tower silhouettes are tested against an independent ray-caster (IoU ≥ 0.99).
  • Sun position is the repository's frozen NOAA convention (UTC+10, no refraction), checked to 1e-9°.
  • Lighting, shadows and ambient occlusion are illustrative. They show the modelled solids under that sun; they are not a shadow study (see /study/) and say nothing about finishes or glazing.
  • Storey lines: E2 at the floor RLs dimensioned on the approved drawings (solid); E1 at inferred equal 3.3 m storeys (dashed).
  • Ground: cadastral parcels and 1 m contours are a cartographic index, not survey and not imagery. The grey plane beyond the terrain edge is outside the model.
  • Massing is schematic: no balustrades, screens, windows, finishes or invented trees.
  • Only W01 is a model-defined source viewpoint; NG55/NG57/NG59 are hypothetical positions.

Guides

The top ribbon shows grid bearings along the horizon; the left scale shows elevation angle above the horizontal through the image centre's bearing. Both are computed from the exact camera model.
