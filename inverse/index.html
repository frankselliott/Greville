How tall would it have to be?

The frozen model asks what the approved buildings take from these backyards. This page asks the question backwards: at a given moment, what is the lowest an envelope could be and still reach this ground at all? That height is the first-contact RL. Where it sits above the bounded comparator family, no building in that family reaches — and the year map below shows every hour of 2026 at once.

This page computes the inverse; it does not restate the frozen results. The forward figures — losses, peaks, otherwise-sunlit areas — are finalised and live in the frozen visualiser. Nothing here alters them. The inverse is also a known open item: it is reproduced from the frozen browser method, which disagrees with the frozen offline first_contact_RL table by up to 0.32 m. What is casting, and where the requirement comes from

What is drawn. The comparator is a bounded family of prisms at RL 46.0, 48.0, 50.0, 52.0 and 55.75, tested because the 2020 approval fixed height as a storey count only — no top RL appears in any source. RL 48.0 is the true eight-storey plate. This is a bounded comparator, never the applicant's model, and must not be described as one.

Method. NOAA geometric solar position, UTC+10, no daylight saving, no refraction — the frozen convention. Geometry in EPSG:7856 minus the local origin. For each sample point in the receptor the ray toward the sun is clipped against each comparator ring by half-plane intersection; the rings are convex, so the clip is exact with no sampling along the ray. The entry distance gives the height a prism must reach to cast that far, and the smallest such height over the receptor is the first-contact RL. Ground is drawn flat; the real site slopes.

The open finding. This reproduces the frozen browser method, which takes a minimum over grid sample points. The frozen offline first_contact_RL table was produced some other way: 16 of its 21 values differ by more than 10 mm, worst 0.32 m, and refining the cell does not close the gap. The original method is lost and has not been reconstructed by fitting to its answers. Nothing reported turns on it — every value on both sides is metres clear of the 46.0–55.75 m family.
