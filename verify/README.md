# Verification pack — Greville shadow model v1.1-AB

For an architect, shadow consultant or surveyor who wants to re-derive these
results independently rather than take them on trust.

Everything in `out/` is generated by `export.py` from the frozen model, with one
exception noted below. Nothing is hand-entered. Re-running `export.py` reproduces
those files byte for byte — checked from a clean extraction of this pack.

The exception is `out/ellivo_registration.json`, which is a *record* of how the
applicant's scanned sheets were registered to this frame. It is an input to the
comparison, not an output of the model, and `export.py` does not regenerate it.

---

## 1. What the model is, in one paragraph

The approved buildings (A006835434) are extruded from their approved
footprints to their approved top RLs and cast onto a ground plane at RL 23.320,
which is the receiver level of Lot 63 SP354191. Shadow loss is measured over
the Cluster 5 backyards of Lots 60–63 SP354191, after subtracting the shade the
dwellings and fences on those lots cast on themselves. Solar position is NOAA
geometric, UTC+10, **no daylight saving and no atmospheric refraction**.

Two independent paths exist in the engine and they are not interchangeable:

| path | function | used for |
|---|---|---|
| per-edge Minkowski sweep | `prism_shadow_parts` | drawing only |
| exact per-point ray test | `occluded_mask` | **every reported number** |

No figure in any output comes from the sweep.

## 2. Check the coordinate frame

```
python3 -I mga.py
```

Self-tests the Krüger n-series transverse Mercator against the test point
published in the GDA2020 Technical Manual (Flinders Peak, MGA zone 55) and by
round trip. Expect agreement to well under a millimetre.

Everything in this pack is **GDA2020 / MGA zone 56, EPSG:7856**. The model's
internal coordinates are that frame minus a local origin of
E 504045, N 6966712 — exact and reversible.

## 3. Check the sun

`out/sun_positions.csv` gives altitude, azimuth, shadow bearing and shadow
length per metre of height, at every instant used anywhere in this work.

Put the site at **latitude −27.422924, longitude 153.041035**, time zone
**UTC+10 with no daylight saving**, and compare against your own solar tool.

Expect your tool to read a few minutes wider at sunrise and sunset than this
model does. That is correct and expected: almanac times include atmospheric
refraction and a sun-radius allowance, this model is geometric centre-of-sun.
21 June solves here to 06:42–16:57 against an almanac 06:36–17:02.

## 4. Load the geometry

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `out/geometry.dxf` | R12 DXF, MGA zone 56 eastings and northings, layered |
| `out/geometry.geojson` | same features; WGS84 lon/lat for GIS, with the authoritative MGA coordinates in each feature's `mga2020_zone56` property |

Layers: `APPROVED`, `COMPARATOR`, `CADASTRE_LOT`, `CADASTRE_PARCEL`,
`RECEPTOR_YARD`, `RECEPTOR_SCHED`, `DWELLING`.

`out/shadows.dxf` and `out/shadows.geojson` carry the computed sweeps at every
instant, layered by instant. **Each feature is one part of the sweep, not the
finished shadow** — the shadow is their union. Areas are not derived from them.

## 5. Check the numbers

```
cd ../model
python3 run_tests.py           # 26 tests against physics and geometry, not against stored output
python3 verify_frozen_peaks.py # every frozen figure, recomputed
```

`out/frozen_peaks_recomputed.csv` puts each frozen peak beside the value this
engine computes for it now. Worst difference across all 24: **0.0012 m²**.

`out/receptor_grid_spec.csv` records each receptor's sample grid — cell size,
point count, and a SHA-256 of the point set. The grid is fully determined by
the receptor polygon and the cell size, so regenerate it with
`Model.grid(position, lot, FROZEN_CELL)` and compare the hash rather than
shipping 685,000 rows.

## 6. The applicant's diagrams

`out/ellivo_registration.json` records how Ellivo DA-600 and DA-601 rev 8 were
registered to this frame, the resulting transform, the cross-checks, and the
caveats. Read the caveats before using the comparison — in particular, their
diagrams shade **every** building while this model's shade is cast by the
approved buildings alone, so gross extents are not like for like.

`out/cluster5_at_applicant_instants.csv` evaluates the Cluster 5 backyards at
each of the applicant's own instants.

## 7. What this pack is not

It is not an expert report and it is not certified. It is a model, its inputs,
its outputs and enough material to disagree with it precisely. Where a choice
could not be resolved from a source document, the model records the choice
rather than silently picking one — see `../model/PROVENANCE.md`.
