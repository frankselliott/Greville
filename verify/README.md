# Verification pack — Greville shadow model v1.1-AB

For an architect, shadow consultant or surveyor who wants to re-derive these
results independently rather than take them on trust.

Everything in `out/` is generated by `export.py` from the frozen model, with one
exception noted below. Nothing is hand-entered. Re-running `export.py` reproduces
those files byte for byte — checked from a clean extraction of this pack.

The exception is `out/ellivo_registration.json`, which is a *record* of how the
applicant's scanned sheets were registered to this frame. It is an input to the
comparison, not an output of the model, and `export.py` does not regenerate it.

The pack itself is built deterministically, so two builds from the same tree
are byte-identical and the download can be hashed meaningfully:

```sh
tar --sort=name --owner=0 --group=0 --numeric-owner \
    --mtime='2026-11-10 00:00:00' -cf - <the 34 files listed in this pack> \
  | gzip -n -9 > greville-verification-pack.tar.gz
```

## 1. What the model is, in one paragraph

The approved buildings (A006835434) are extruded from their approved
footprints to their approved top RLs and cast onto a ground plane at RL 23.320,
which is the receiver level of Lot 63 SP354191. Shadow loss is measured over
the Cluster 5 backyards of Lots 60–63 SP354191, after subtracting the shade the
dwellings and fences on those lots cast on themselves. Solar position is NOAA
geometric, UTC+10, **no daylight saving and no atmospheric refraction**.

Two independent paths exist in the engine and they are not interchangeable:

| path | function | used for |
|---|---|---|
| per-edge Minkowski sweep | `prism_shadow_parts` | drawing only |
| exact per-point ray test | `occluded_mask` | **every reported number** |

No figure in any output comes from the sweep.

## 2. Check the coordinate frame

```
python3 -I mga.py
```

Self-tests the Krüger n-series transverse Mercator against the test point
published in the GDA2020 Technical Manual (Flinders Peak, MGA zone 55) and by
round trip. Expect agreement to well under a millimetre.

Everything in this pack is **GDA2020 / MGA zone 56, EPSG:7856**. The model's
internal coordinates are that frame minus a local origin of
E 504045, N 6966712 — exact and reversible.

## 3. Check the sun

`out/sun_positions.csv` gives altitude, azimuth, shadow bearing and shadow
length per metre of height, at every instant used anywhere in this work.

Put the site at **latitude −27.422924, longitude 153.041035**, time zone
**UTC+10 with no daylight saving**, and compare against your own solar tool.

Expect your tool to read a few minutes wider at sunrise and sunset than this
model does. That is correct and expected: published sunrise and sunset include
atmospheric refraction and a sun-radius allowance, while this model is
geometric centre-of-sun.

On 21 June 2026 this model puts the sun above the horizon from **06:42**, with
**16:57** the last lit minute and the centre crossing during 16:58. Solving the
same routine for the conventional definition instead — upper limb on the
horizon, centre at −0°50′, being 34′ of refraction plus 16′ of semi-diameter —
gives **06:38 to 17:02**.

Both of those are computed here, by the routine in `../model/greville_model/
solar.py`. Neither is quoted from a published almanac, and checking them
against one is a reasonable thing to do. The four-minute spread at each end is
the whole of the difference, and it is why a diagram timed at 17:00 on 21 June
is not drawing an impossible shadow even though this model's clock stops at
16:57.

## 4. Load the geometry

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `out/geometry.dxf` | R12 DXF, MGA zone 56 eastings and northings, layered |
| `out/geometry.geojson` | same features; WGS84 lon/lat for GIS, with the authoritative MGA coordinates in each feature's `mga2020_zone56` property |

Layers: `APPROVED`, `COMPARATOR_ENVELOPE`, `CADASTRE_LOT`, `CADASTRE_PARCEL`,
`RECEPTOR_YARD`, `RECEPTOR_SCHED`, `DWELLING`.

The eight-storey comparator is Buildings A and B on their approved footprints
(`APPROVED`, `building_A` and `building_B_plate`) cut to the comparator RL:
that is what the sealed incremental table was computed against, and what
`Model.comparator_casters()` and the Study page use. `COMPARATOR_ENVELOPE`
carries the rectangular envelopes the frozen browser page draws for its live
comparator instead; they give more affected days than the sealed table (see
`../model/PROVENANCE.md`, *The two comparators*). The `SHADOW_COMPARATOR_RL48_*`
layers of `shadows.dxf` are cast by the cut footprints.

`out/shadows.dxf` and `out/shadows.geojson` carry the computed sweeps at every
instant, layered by instant. **Each feature is one part of the sweep, not the
finished shadow** — the shadow is their union. Areas are not derived from them.

### In 3D, for Revit and other CAD

`out/geometry.dxf` is flat outlines. `out_revit/` holds the same model in 3D:

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `out_revit/greville_revit_mga.dxf` | AutoCAD 2000 DXF, units declared as metres, MGA zone 56 eastings and northings, heights in m AHD |
| `out_revit/greville_revit_local.dxf` | identical geometry near the origin: local = MGA − (E 504045, N 6966712) |
| `out_revit/README.txt` | layers, import settings, and what is left out and why |

The approved buildings and the Lot 60–63 dwellings are closed solids at their
model levels, the boundary fences are 1.5 m panels, and the backyards and lots
are outlines at receiver level. In Revit: Insert → Link CAD, import units
Meter. Place the local file *Origin to Internal Origin*; place the MGA file
*Center to Center*, then Manage → Coordinates → Acquire Coordinates, because
MGA coordinates are thousands of kilometres from Revit's origin.

The approved buildings are drawn from the approved ground floor (RL 21.600);
the model extrudes them from RL 0, and any base below the lowest receiver level
(RL 23.320) casts exactly the same shadow on the backyards. The eight-storey
comparator is on `COMPARATOR_RL48` and `COMPARATOR_RL55_75`: Buildings A and B
on their approved footprints, cut to that RL, as the sealed incremental table
used. Turn the approved layers off to see it alone.

Checked: every solid is closed with outward faces, each volume equals footprint
area × height to 1e-10, every footprint vertex matches the model, and the two
files differ by exactly the stated offset. `export_revit.py` regenerates
`out_revit/` byte for byte, but unlike everything else here it needs one
package: `pip install ezdxf==1.4.5`.

## 5. Check the numbers

```
cd ../model
python3 run_tests.py           # 29 tests against physics and geometry, not against stored output
python3 verify_frozen_peaks.py # every frozen figure, recomputed
```

Three of those 29 are worth naming, because they are the ones that do not
depend on the original source at all — which matters, because the engine is
the one part of the model that §5a does not check against the sealed release
(see `model/PROVENANCE.md`). `tests/test_sweep_vs_ray.py`
compares the two shadow paths against each other over tens of thousands of
samples. They are independent derivations: the sweep translates a footprint
along the anti-solar bearing and bridges each edge, the ray test asks whether
a segment toward the sun meets the footprint. `PROVENANCE.md` asserted they
agree; nothing checked it. They do, and the test is strict about *how*: every
disagreement must sit on the shadow boundary, where a finite grid cannot
resolve which side a point falls. A single disagreement away from the edge
fails the test, because that would be a model split rather than quantisation.

## 5a. Check against the sealed release

The reconstruction can be checked against the sealed v1.1-AB release itself,
not only against the frozen page that embedded it.

```
cd ../verify
python3 diff_original.py /path/to/sealed-archive.tar.gz   # whole release
python3 diff_original.py /path/to/model-data/             # or just the data
```

It takes either form, and says which it got:

* **The sealed release.** If the input hashes to `6df4dde6…c76d4f0` it is
  identified as the sealed v1.1-AB archive and its own 47-file manifest is
  verified end to end.
* **The model data alone** — a directory or tarball holding `geometry/`
  and `outputs/`. Every file supplied is checked against a SHA-256 pinned in
  this script's own source (`SEALED_FILE_HASHES`), not against anything
  shipped with the data, so a manifest sent alongside it is a convenience
  rather than the root of trust: regenerating one to match an altered file
  does not help. Files not supplied are reported as not supplied, and any
  check whose inputs are absent is skipped rather than failed.

A file that is present but does not match its sealed hash fails, in either
mode. That is the property worth having: the numbers can be proved to be the
model's own, by anyone, without trusting whoever sent them.

The model's data files are downloadable from the study page as
`greville_model_data_v1.1-AB.tar.gz` (8 KB), together with a manifest of their
SHA-256 values; run the script on that. The complete release is not
distributed.

It checks twelve things and prints each: the archive's own 47-file manifest;
the four approved footprints vertex by vertex and their `top_rl`; their areas;
the eight receptor polygons; the 24 receptor metadata values; the casting edge;
the 24 frozen peak figures embedded in the page; the 24 governing instants; the
8-entry attribution split; the 24-row full-year table; the 16-row incremental
table; and the 21-row offline `first_contact_RL` table recomputed by the
original's own hull method.

That covers all five frozen tables the page carries, not a sample, and the
full-year and incremental checks assert their own row coverage so a parser that
matched nothing would fail rather than pass. Current result: all twelve pass —
geometry identical to 0.067 mm, receptor metadata exact to full float
precision, the embedded peak figures bit-identical to the release's, and every
field of every results table matching.

What it does **not** establish is the engine implementation: the
reconstruction's solar and shadow code is not compared with the original
source, and the independent tests in §5 are what stands behind the physics
there. Nor does it check the receiver levels, which v1.1-AB carries forward
from v1.0.2. The script says both when it finishes.

### The Study page has its own gate now

```
cd ../verify
python3 gate_study.py                       # in the repository: checks ../study/index.html
python3 gate_study.py path/to/index.html    # from this pack: the Study page saved from the site
```

It needs node with playwright. The Study page is not part of this pack; save it
from the site (it is one self-contained HTML file) and pass its path.

`PROVENANCE.md` says of the Study page: *"If its figures are ever to be relied
on, it needs its own gate."* This is it. The Study is not the frozen model — it
credits casters the frozen model did not and samples at a coarser cell — so it
cannot be checked by equality. What it can be checked against is the claim its
own README makes: adding a caster can only reduce a loss, never raise it. The
gate drives the live page in a browser, reads the figures it actually renders,
and fails if any exceeds the frozen loss by more than the sampling allowance —
for the approved scheme and for the comparator at RL 48.0 and RL 55.75, 144
comparisons in all.

It gates a direction, not a value. It does not make the Study figures the
frozen figures; those remain the ones in `model/`.

`out/frozen_peaks_recomputed.csv` puts each frozen peak beside the value this
engine computes for it now. Worst difference across all 24: **0.0012 m²**.

`out/receptor_grid_spec.csv` records each receptor's sample grid — cell size,
point count, and a SHA-256 of the point set. The grid is fully determined by
the receptor polygon and the cell size, so regenerate it with
`Model.grid(position, lot, FROZEN_CELL)` and compare the hash rather than
shipping 685,000 rows.

## 6. The applicant's diagrams

`out/ellivo_registration.json` records how Ellivo DA-600 and DA-601 rev 8 were
registered to this frame, the resulting transform, the cross-checks, and the
caveats. Read the caveats before using the comparison — in particular, their
diagrams shade **every** building while this model's shade is cast by the
approved buildings alone, so gross extents are not like for like.

`out/cluster5_at_applicant_instants.csv` evaluates the Cluster 5 backyards at
each of the applicant's own instants.

## 7. What this pack is not

It is not an expert report and it is not certified. It is a model, its inputs,
its outputs and enough material to disagree with it precisely. Where a choice
could not be resolved from a source document, the model records the choice
rather than silently picking one — see `../model/PROVENANCE.md`.
